Logo

Logo guidelines →

The mark is a three-lobed interlaced knot: one band in Devil blue, one shading to silver, one to ivory. It is a rendered, dimensional image, not flat vector art. Never recolor it, flatten it, outline it, or set it inside a box. In web chrome, pair the transparent mark with the HTML wordmark (shown in the header above) instead of placing the round-text lockup at small sizes.

Voice

Voice and writing →

Write like this

  • “100% of your gift will go to Stanton.”
  • “Teachers who guide beautiful minds deserve a beautiful space!”
  • “Stanton is, and always will be, your home.”
  • Name the thing: the faculty lounge, the auditorium shades, the Phoenix Fund.

Not like this

  • “Empowering tomorrow's leaders through transformative giving.”
  • “Your support makes a difference.” (Which support? What difference?)
  • Urgency the facts don't support: “Last chance!” when it isn't.
  • Figures you can't source.
