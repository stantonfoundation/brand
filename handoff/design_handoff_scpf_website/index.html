Monthly

For faculty & parents

Any Stanton teacher, department, or parent organization may request funding. Awards are announced before each semester begins so you can plan with certainty.

Start a grant request
01
Submit a one-page request

What you need, what it costs, and how many students it reaches. No narrative essays.

02
Board review

October and March cycles. The principal's office reviews for feasibility; the board votes.

03
Funds released directly

Paid to the vendor or reimbursed within two weeks. One short report at year end.
