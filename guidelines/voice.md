# Voice and writing

The Foundation sounds like proud alumni and parents talking to people who love the same school: warm, specific, confident, plain.

## Tagline

**Generations With One Common Goal.** Used as the hero eyebrow and on the social card. In running text, write it in sentence case: "generations with one common goal".

## How we write

- **Be specific.** Name the project, the amount, the date. "The Foundation refurbished the faculty lounge with $15K in seed funding" beats "Your support makes a difference."
- **Lead with the school.** Stanton's record (National Blue Ribbon School in 1987, 2018 and 2024; #1 Duval County public high school in U.S. News for nearly two decades) is the case. State it plainly.
- **Ask clearly, once.** One giving action per section, in gold, with a verb: "Give to Stanton".
- **Warm, not breathless.** One exclamation mark per page is plenty. "Teachers who guide beautiful minds deserve a beautiful space!" earns its exclamation mark.
- **Every dollar claim must be true.** "100% of your gift will go to Stanton" is a promise. Do not print figures, donor counts or deadlines you cannot source.
- **Plain words.** "Give", not "make a philanthropic investment". "Help pay for", not "underwrite", outside the mission statement.

## Mechanics

- Sentence case for headings and buttons. Title Case only for proper names of programs and campaigns: Phoenix Fund, 'Welcome Home' Match.
- Numerals for all figures: $15K in stats, $15,000 in sentences; 1,200+ students.
- School years with an en dash: 2026–27.
- Use the serial comma.
- Link text says where it goes and ends with an arrow: "See our wish list →".

## Examples

From the live site, in voice:

- "Stanton is, and always will be, your home."
- "No gift given from the heart is small! Our Blue Devils appreciate support at all levels."
- "The Foundation's mission is to underwrite Stanton's academic, capital improvement, arts, and extracurricular initiatives that are not included in the school district's budget."

Off voice:

- "Empowering tomorrow's leaders through transformative giving."
- "Don't miss your chance to make an impact!"
