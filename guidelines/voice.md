# Voice and writing

The Foundation writes like alumni and parents talking to people who love the same school. The copy on the live site is warm and specific, and it is proud of Stanton without overselling it.

The rules below come from that copy. When this page and the live site disagree, the site is right and this page needs fixing.

## How the site writes

- **Specific.** It names the project, the amount and the season. "As a pilot project in Summer 2026, the Foundation secured $15K in seed funding and refurbished Stanton's faculty lounge." A line like "Your support makes a difference" says none of that.
- **The school's record makes the case.** National Blue Ribbon School in 1987, 2018 and 2024. Recognized by U.S. News & World Report for nearly two decades as the #1 Duval County public high school. The site states these plainly and lets them carry the argument.
- **One clear ask.** Each section has at most one giving action, in gold, with a verb: "Give to Stanton".
- **Warm.** "Teachers who guide beautiful minds deserve a beautiful space!" and "No gift given from the heart is small!" Exclamation marks are fine where the warmth is real. Don't add them to manufacture urgency.
- **True.** "100% of your gift will go to Stanton" is a promise. Do not publish a figure, donor count or deadline you cannot source.

## Mechanics

- Section headings are in sentence case: "What your gift funds", "Ways to give".
- Program and campaign names keep their own capitals: Phoenix Fund, 'Welcome Home' Match.
- Use numerals for figures: $15K in a stat, $15,000 in a sentence, 1,200+ students.
- Use the serial comma, as the mission statement does.
- Link text says where it goes and ends with an arrow: "See our wish list →".

## Examples from the live site

- "Stanton is, and always will be, your home."
- "No gift given from the heart is small! Our Blue Devils appreciate support at all levels."
- "The Foundation's mission is to underwrite Stanton's academic, capital improvement, arts, and extracurricular initiatives that are not included in the school district's budget."

The site does not write like this:

- "Empowering tomorrow's leaders through transformative giving."
- "Don't miss your chance to make an impact!"
