# Photography

Photos show real Stanton life: students, teachers, alumni, the campus, the work the Foundation pays for. No stock photography.

## What to shoot and choose

- People doing things: rehearsing, competing, building, teaching. Not posed rows facing the camera, except board portraits.
- The thing a gift bought, in use: the faculty lounge with teachers in it, the auditorium during a show.
- Natural light, honest color. No heavy filters, vignettes or HDR.
- Several generations where possible. The Foundation's line is "Generations with one common goal".

## Treatment

- **Photo reel:** photos run in black and white (applied as a CSS filter, so the files stay in color) and return to color when hovered. The reel sits on midnight with 6px gaps.
- **Board portraits:** head and shoulders, plain background, same crop across the set.
- **Social share image:** `assets/social/og-default.jpg`, 1200×630: the muted mark on navy, the eyebrow, the name in Newsreader, and the line "Supporting Stanton's students, faculty and school."
- Never put text over a busy photo. If text must sit on an image, the area behind it must be a calm dark field, or the text goes in a navy band below.

## Files

- Web: WebP, long edge 1600px or less for full images and around 400px for thumbnails. The reel uses 240px tall images (160px below 960px).
- Every published image needs alt text that says what is in it ("Students rehearsing on the auditorium stage"), or `alt=""` when it is purely decorative, as reel photos are.

## Consent

Most subjects are minors. Use only photos the school has cleared for publication under its media-release process, and do not name students in captions without that clearance.
