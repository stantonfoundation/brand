Logo

Logo guidelines →

Do not recolor, outline, stretch or box the knot. Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the knot's width on every side. Minimum sizes: the knot alone 32px tall on screen; the full lockup 120px wide on screen, or 1 inch in print. More files, including muted and transparent versions, are in assets/logo/.

Page ground

Roughly 60% paper and white, 26% navy, 9% silver and steel, 5% gold. Gold means "give". Use it for giving actions and little else, and never for small text on a light ground.

For print, use the CMYK values and let the paper stand in for the page ground. Field border is a screen color for form fields and has no print value.
